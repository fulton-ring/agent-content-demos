## Never put into an AI tool

**Bright Futures After School**

- These stay out of AI tools unless a tool is approved for them above: student names, photos, video, grades, test scores, attendance, IEP and 504 details, allergies, medications, custody and pickup information, incident reports, donor names and spreadsheet, staff and volunteer details, passwords and login details. Our district data-sharing agreement covers student records.

**Cedar Valley Family Center**

- Case files (county contract): names, addresses, income, immigration status, domestic violence, mental health and substance use notes. Student records (district agreement): names, grades, scores, attendance. Donor notes. Staff files. Passwords and login details.

**Eastside Community Health Clinic**

- Diagnoses, medications, behavioral health notes, substance use treatment records, HIV status, reproductive health, immigration status, sliding fee income documents, insurance numbers, staff files, the donor list, and passwords. HIPAA covers patient records; substance use records carry extra federal protection.

**Northgate Community Food Bank**

- SNAP applicants' Social Security numbers, income, household members and immigration documents (state human services agreement); donor names and giving records in Salesforce; volunteer names, phone numbers, emergency contacts and court-ordered case details; mobile pantry sign-up names and zip codes; passwords and logins.

## Off limits

**Bright Futures After School**

- Mandated reporting. If you suspect abuse, you call it in. AI never touches it.
- Never paste incident reports or notes about a child's behavior into AI.
- No AI-generated images of our kids.
- No AI-edited photos of real kids.

**Cedar Valley Family Center**

- Installing a browser plugin that reads your email.
- Using AI to score intake essays or place adult students in class levels.
- AI-written public statements about the news, or an AI photo passed off as one of our families.

**Eastside Community Health Clinic**

- Nobody uses AI to make clinical decisions.
- Nobody uses AI to decide sliding fee eligibility.

**Northgate Community Food Bank**

- No SNAP application information goes into any AI tool.
- No AI answers SNAP eligibility questions. A person answers the SNAP line.
- No AI images of hungry people. Our photos are real, with consent forms.

## Decisions about people

**Bright Futures After School**

- AI never decides who gets services, a place in our program, or a consequence for a child.
- Staff write incident reports and notes about a child's behavior themselves.

**Cedar Valley Family Center**

- AI never decides who gets emergency assistance funds. Caseworkers and their supervisor decide. AI may only help with wording or ideas.

**Eastside Community Health Clinic**

- AI may help look something up or draft a note. A person decides.

**Northgate Community Food Bank**

- AI never decides who gets help, benefits or a volunteer role. Trained people decide.

## Being open about AI

**Bright Futures After School**

- Translated newsletters say they were machine-translated, so parents know to ask if something looks wrong.
- We tell parents about any AI use with their kids.

**Cedar Valley Family Center**

- A person signs thank-you letters. Label materials AI helped make, like reading passages. Answer funders honestly when they ask about AI.

**Eastside Community Health Clinic**

- Tell patients when the scribe is listening. They can say no.
- Reviewed handouts need no AI label. Chatbots must say they are not people.

**Northgate Community Food Bank**

- We follow the AI clause in our corporate partner's agreement.
- If a funder or partner asks about our AI use, we answer honestly.
