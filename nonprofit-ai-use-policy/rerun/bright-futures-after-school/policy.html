Bright Futures After School AI use policy

Draft for board review · Effective when the board approves it · Owner: Renee, executive director · Next review: in 12 months

Why we have this policy. We want AI to save time on activity plans and paperwork. We must keep children's information safe and follow our district agreement.

Who it covers. Staff, group leaders, volunteers, contractors and board members at all four sites.

Tools we use

  • No AI tool is approved yet.
  • New tools, add-ons and AI features. Renee approves any new tool, plugin, agent or AI feature in software we already use. Joy checks settings first. The district must also agree if student data is involved.
  • Accounts. Use a work account where you have one, and turn off any setting that lets the vendor train on what you type.

What you can use AI for

  • Everyone, now. Use any AI tool, signed in with a work email, for work with no information about a real child, family, donor or staff member.
  • Group leaders: activity plans and activity ideas.
  • Group leaders: STEM projects and games.
  • Site directors: drafting schedules and the weekly parent newsletter.

Needs approval first

  • Grant reports and outcomes data for the state or funders — Renee, executive director
  • Student information, only inside the district platform's own tools — Renee, plus the district

Off limits

  • Never put this into an AI tool unless the tool is approved for it above: These stay out of AI tools unless a tool is approved for them above: student names, photos, video, grades, test scores, attendance, IEP and 504 details, allergies, medications, custody and pickup information, incident reports, donor names and spreadsheet, staff and volunteer details, passwords and login details. Our district data-sharing agreement covers student records.
  • Mandated reporting. If you suspect abuse, you call it in. AI never touches it.
  • Never paste incident reports or notes about a child's behavior into AI.
  • No AI-generated images of our kids.
  • No AI-edited photos of real kids.

Check everything before it goes out

  • You are responsible for anything you send, even if AI wrote it.
  • Check facts, names, times, dates and numbers. A wrong pickup time can leave a child waiting.
  • A person checks grant report numbers against our records before anything goes to the state.

Decisions about people

  • AI never decides who gets services, a place in our program, or a consequence for a child.
  • Staff write incident reports and notes about a child's behavior themselves.

Being open about AI

  • Translated newsletters say they were machine-translated, so parents know to ask if something looks wrong.
  • We tell parents about any AI use with their kids.

If something goes wrong

  • Tell your site director the same day. Site directors tell Renee the same day.
  • Reporting a mistake is expected. Nobody is punished for it.

Who owns this policy

  • The owner makes sure staff and volunteers see this policy.
  • We review it every 12 months.

Open questions for the board

  • Can kids use AI during homework hour, and do we follow each school's rule or make our own?
  • Which AI tools, if any, will we approve or pay for, such as Gemini or ChatGPT, and do group leaders have work emails?
  • May we use AI to translate the newsletter, and who checks the Vietnamese version before it goes out?
  • Should the board approve this policy and later changes?
