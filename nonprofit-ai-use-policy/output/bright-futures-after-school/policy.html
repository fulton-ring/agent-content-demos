Bright Futures After School AI use policy

Draft for board review · Effective when the board approves it · Owner: Executive director (Renee) · Next review: In 12 months

Why we have this policy. We want AI to take paperwork and planning off your plate. We must protect the children in our care and keep our promise to the district.

Who it covers. Staff, group leaders, volunteers, contractors and board members.

Tools we use

  • No AI tool is approved yet.
  • New tools, add-ons and AI features. Renee approves any new tool, plugin or AI feature in existing software. Joy checks the settings first. If student data is involved, the district must agree too.
  • Accounts. Use a work account where we have one, and turn off any setting that lets the vendor train on what you type.

What you can use AI for

  • Everyone, now. You may use any AI tool, through a work email, for work with no information about a real person in it.
  • Group leaders: activity ideas, STEM projects and games.
  • Site directors: schedules and weekly reports with no child or family details.
  • Site directors: drafting the weekly parent newsletter.
  • Translating the newsletter into Spanish and Vietnamese.

Needs approval first

  • Grant reports or outcomes data for the state or funders — Your manager
  • AI-drafted messages to parents beyond the weekly newsletter — Your manager

Off limits

  • Never put this into an AI tool unless the tool is approved for it above: Children's names, photos and video, grades and test scores, attendance, IEP and 504 information, allergies and medications, custody and pickup orders, and behavior incident reports. Also donor names and contact details, staff and volunteer information, and all passwords and logins. Our district data-sharing agreement keeps student records in the district platform.
  • Never put an incident report, or a note about a child's behavior, into AI, even to clean it up.
  • Never use AI for mandated reporting. If you suspect abuse, call it in yourself.
  • Never make AI images of our kids, or use AI to edit photos of real kids.

Check everything before it goes out

  • Whoever sends AI-assisted work is responsible for it. Check facts, names, numbers, dates and tone first.
  • Check every pickup time and date in a translated newsletter. Grant report numbers must match our records.

Decisions about people

  • AI never decides a child's placement, discipline, accommodations or pickup, or who we hire. People decide. AI may only help with general planning, like activity ideas and schedules.

Being open about AI

  • Machine-translated newsletters say so, so parents know to ask if something looks wrong.
  • We will tell parents before AI is used with their children in our program.

If something goes wrong

  • Tell your site director the same day. Site directors tell Renee the same day.
  • Reporting a mistake, like pasting in a child's information, is expected and never punished.

Who owns this policy

  • Renee owns this policy day to day and shares it with all staff and volunteers.
  • Joy checks tool settings. We review this policy in 12 months.

Open questions for the board

  • Do children use AI during homework hour, and do we follow each school's rule or make our own? (Board and principals)
  • Will the board approve and pay for any AI tool, such as Gemini in Google Workspace or ChatGPT?
  • How will we learn how our grant writer uses AI, and what must they follow?
  • Who will check the Vietnamese translation of the newsletter before it goes out?
