Bright Futures After School AI use policy

Draft for board review · Effective when the board approves it · Owner: Renee, executive director · Next review: In 12 months

Why we have this policy. We want AI to save time on activity plans and paperwork. We must protect children's information and families' trust.

Who it covers. Staff, group leaders, volunteers, contractors and board members, on any device or account.

Tools we use

  • ChatGPT (personal or free accounts): Activity ideas, newsletter drafts and translation only. No protected information, ever.
  • New tools, add-ons and AI features. Renee approves new tools, extensions and AI features. Joy checks settings first. Student data also needs the district's agreement.

What you can use AI for

  • Activity ideas, STEM projects and games
  • Drafting the weekly parent newsletter, with no child names
  • Translating the newsletter, labeled as machine-translated
  • Schedules and weekly reports, with no child details

Needs approval first

  • Anything touching student data — Renee, plus the district
  • AI help with state grant reports — Renee
  • Children using AI in our program — Renee

Off limits

  • Never put this into an AI tool unless the tool is approved for it above: Children's names, photos and video, grades, test scores, attendance, IEP and 504 information, allergies and medications, custody orders, pickup lists and incident reports. Also the donor list and staff and volunteer personal details.
  • Never paste or photograph child information, even a homework page with a name. Never "clean up" incident reports with AI.
  • Suspected abuse is reported by phone, never through AI.
  • No AI-made images of our kids. No AI-edited photos of real kids.

Check everything before it goes out

  • You are responsible for anything you send, even if AI wrote it.
  • Check facts, names, dates, times and numbers against our records. A wrong pickup time hurts a child.
  • Have a fluent reader check translations before they go out.

Decisions about people

  • AI never decides discipline, accommodations, pickup or program placement. Staff decide.

Being open about AI

  • Label translated newsletters as machine-translated, so parents know to ask if something looks wrong.
  • Tell parents before children use AI in our program.

If something goes wrong

  • Tell your site director the same day. Site directors tell Renee the same day.
  • Reporting a mistake is expected and never punished.

Who owns this policy

  • Renee owns this policy. Joy, operations manager, checks tool settings.
  • Renee shares it with staff and group leaders when they start. We review it yearly.

Open questions for the board

  • Homework hour: may kids use AI, and do we follow each school's rule or make our own? Board and principals should decide.
  • Who checks the Vietnamese translation of the newsletter?
  • May office staff use Gemini in Google Workspace, and how do we make our contract grant writer follow this policy?
  • Does the board approve this policy and later changes, and must we tell the district if student data enters an AI tool?
