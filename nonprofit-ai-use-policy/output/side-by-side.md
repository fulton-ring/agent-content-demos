# How the policies differ

Generated by `uv run build-policies` from `output/*/policy.json`.

## Tools we use

**Bright Futures After School**

- **ChatGPT (personal or free accounts)**: Activity ideas, newsletter drafts and translation only. No protected information, ever.

**Cedar Valley Family Center**

- **Microsoft Copilot (pilot)**: Drafting appeals, thank-yous and grant summaries. No protected information.
- **ChatGPT (free or personal accounts)**: Lesson plans, reading passages, practice questions, translations. No protected information.
- **Canva AI images**: Images for communications. No protected information.

**Eastside Community Health Clinic**

- **AI scribe from our health record vendor**: Visit notes for the three pilot providers; signed business associate agreement, so it may hold patient information.
- **OpenEvidence and UpToDate AI search**: Clinical questions only; no patient information.
- **ChatGPT**: General letters and handouts; no patient, donor or staff information, even names.

**Northgate Community Food Bank**

- **Google Gemini (in Google Workspace)**: Drafting in Gmail and Docs. No protected information.
- **ChatGPT (personal paid account)**: Volunteer newsletters and social posts only. No protected information.
- **Claude**: Grant first drafts only. No protected information.

## Never put into an AI tool

**Bright Futures After School**

- Children's names, photos and video, grades, test scores, attendance, IEP and 504 information, allergies and medications, custody orders, pickup lists and incident reports. Also the donor list and staff and volunteer personal details.

**Cedar Valley Family Center**

- Case files (names, addresses, income, immigration status, children, domestic violence, mental health and substance use notes); donor names, giving history, gift notes; student names, scores, grades, attendance; staff HR files; volunteer details. Our county contract and district agreement require this.

**Eastside Community Health Clinic**

- Diagnoses, medications, behavioral health notes, substance use treatment records, HIV status, reproductive health, immigration status, sliding fee income documents, insurance numbers, staff files, and the donor list (some donors are patients).

**Northgate Community Food Bank**

- SNAP applicants' Social Security numbers, income, household members and immigration documents. Donor records in Salesforce. Volunteer names, phone numbers and emergency contacts. Court-ordered volunteers' case details. Mobile pantry sign-up names and zip codes. No tool above is approved for any of this.

## Off limits

**Bright Futures After School**

- Never paste or photograph child information, even a homework page with a name. Never "clean up" incident reports with AI.
- Suspected abuse is reported by phone, never through AI.
- No AI-made images of our kids. No AI-edited photos of real kids.

**Cedar Valley Family Center**

- No AI photos passed off as real families.
- People, not AI, write public statements about the news.

**Eastside Community Health Clinic**

- Never put patient information into ChatGPT, Google Translate or any tool not approved for it above.
- Don't use AI in secret. If you're unsure, ask your manager.

**Northgate Community Food Bank**

- Never paste a SNAP application into any AI tool, even to help.
- No chatbot answers SNAP eligibility questions. A person answers the SNAP line.
- No AI images of hungry people. Our photos are real, with consent forms.

## Decisions about people

**Bright Futures After School**

- AI never decides discipline, accommodations, pickup or program placement. Staff decide.

**Cedar Valley Family Center**

- AI never decides emergency fund awards. Caseworkers and supervisors do.
- AI never scores intake essays or places students in class levels.

**Eastside Community Health Clinic**

- A clinician decides and signs all care, and a person decides sliding fee eligibility. AI may only look things up or draft for review.

**Northgate Community Food Bank**

- AI never decides who gets food, SNAP help, a volunteer role or a consequence. A person decides.
- Only trained, certified outreach staff give SNAP eligibility information. AI does not replace them.

## Being open about AI

**Bright Futures After School**

- Label translated newsletters as machine-translated, so parents know to ask if something looks wrong.
- Tell parents before children use AI in our program.

**Cedar Valley Family Center**

- A thank-you a person signed needs no AI note.
- We label AI-made images and teaching materials.
- We answer funders honestly about AI, including grant writing.

**Eastside Community Health Clinic**

- Tell patients when the scribe is listening and let them say no, using the verbal consent script.
- If we add a chatbot, patients must be told it is not a person.

**Northgate Community Food Bank**

- Check partner agreements for AI clauses and follow them. One partner already has one.
- If a donor, funder or partner asks how we use AI, we tell them honestly.
