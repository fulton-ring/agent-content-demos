## Never put into an AI tool

**Bright Futures After School**

- Children's names, photos and video, grades and test scores, attendance, IEP and 504 information, allergies and medications, custody and pickup orders, and behavior incident reports. Also donor names and contact details, staff and volunteer information, and all passwords and logins. Our district data-sharing agreement keeps student records in the district platform.

**Cedar Valley Family Center**

- Case files: names, addresses, income, immigration status, domestic violence history, mental health and substance use notes (county contract). Student names, grades, scores, attendance (district agreement). Donor giving history and gift notes. Staff and HR files. Passwords and logins.

**Eastside Community Health Clinic**

- Unless a tool above is approved for it: diagnoses, medications, behavioral health notes, HIV status and reproductive health (HIPAA). Substance use treatment records, with extra federal protection. Immigration status, sliding fee income documents, insurance numbers, staff files, the donor list, and all passwords.

**Northgate Community Food Bank**

- Passwords and login details. SNAP outreach records: Social Security numbers, income, household members, immigration documents (state human services agreement). Donor records in Salesforce. Volunteer names, phone numbers, emergency contacts, court-ordered service case details. Mobile pantry sign-ups with names and zip codes.

## Off limits

**Bright Futures After School**

- Never put an incident report, or a note about a child's behavior, into AI, even to clean it up.
- Never use AI for mandated reporting. If you suspect abuse, call it in yourself.
- Never make AI images of our kids, or use AI to edit photos of real kids.

**Cedar Valley Family Center**

- Using AI to decide emergency fund awards or class placement, or to score intake essays.
- Passing off an AI-generated photo as a real family.
- Browser plugins that read your email.
- AI writing public statements. A person writes them.

**Eastside Community Health Clinic**

- No tool that could touch patient information without a business associate agreement.
- AI never makes a clinical decision or signs a note.

**Northgate Community Food Bank**

- No AI chatbot answers eligibility questions or replaces SNAP staff.
- No AI-made images of hungry people. Our photos are real, with consent.

## Decisions about people

**Bright Futures After School**

- AI never decides a child's placement, discipline, accommodations or pickup, or who we hire. People decide. AI may only help with general planning, like activity ideas and schedules.

**Cedar Valley Family Center**

- People decide who gets services, money, a job or a program place. AI may help prepare, never decide.

**Eastside Community Health Clinic**

- A person, not AI, decides sliding fee eligibility and who gets services. AI may help a clinician look things up.

**Northgate Community Food Bank**

- AI never decides who gets food, SNAP help or a volunteer role. A person decides.
- Trained, certified outreach staff answer all eligibility questions.

## Being open about AI

**Bright Futures After School**

- Machine-translated newsletters say so, so parents know to ask if something looks wrong.
- We will tell parents before AI is used with their children in our program.

**Cedar Valley Family Center**

- A thank-you letter needs no AI note if a person signed it and meant it. Label AI-generated materials. Tell funders honestly how we use AI.

**Eastside Community Health Clinic**

- Tell patients when the scribe is listening, and let them say no.
- If we add a patient chatbot, it must say it is not a person.

**Northgate Community Food Bank**

- If asked, we say honestly how we use AI. SNAP line callers always reach a person.
