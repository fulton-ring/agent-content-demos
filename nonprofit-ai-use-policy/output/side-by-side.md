## Never put into an AI tool

**Bright Futures After School**

- Kids' names, photos and video; grades and test scores; attendance; IEP and 504 information; allergies and medications; custody and pickup details; incident reports; the donor list; passwords and logins.

**Cedar Valley Family Center**

- Case files: family names, addresses, income, immigration status, children's names, domestic violence, mental health and substance use notes (county contract). Donor names, giving history, major-gift notes. Student names, test scores, immigration status. Tutoring kids' names, grades, attendance (district agreement). Staff HR files. Passwords and logins.

**Eastside Community Health Clinic**

- Passwords and logins never go in. Nothing else goes in unless the tool is approved above. Covered by HIPAA: diagnoses, medications, behavioral health notes, HIV status, reproductive health, immigration status, insurance numbers. Also substance use treatment records (extra federal protection), income documents, staff files, donor lists.

**Northgate Community Food Bank**

- SNAP applicants' Social Security numbers, income, household members and immigration papers (state human services agreement); Salesforce donor records; volunteer contact and emergency details; court-ordered volunteers' case details; mobile pantry names and zip codes; staff records; passwords and logins.

## Off limits

**Bright Futures After School**

- Never use AI for mandated reporting. If you suspect abuse, call it in.
- No AI-made images of our kids, and no AI-edited photos of real kids.
- Never paste a child's information into AI. Our district agreement keeps student records in the platform.

**Cedar Valley Family Center**

- Browser plugins that read your email.
- Passing off AI-made images as real families or clients.
- AI writing public statements about the news. A person writes them.

**Eastside Community Health Clinic**

- No patient information in any tool without a signed business associate agreement. No exceptions.
- Never let AI make a clinical decision. A clinician decides and signs.

**Northgate Community Food Bank**

- No AI chatbot answers SNAP eligibility questions. A person answers the SNAP line.
- No AI images of hungry people. Our photos are real.

## Decisions about people

**Bright Futures After School**

- AI never decides who gets a place, an accommodation, or a consequence for a child.
- AI may help plan general activities. It does not judge or write about one child.

**Cedar Valley Family Center**

- AI never decides who gets emergency assistance funds. Caseworkers decide with their supervisor.
- AI does not decide who gets a job, services or a consequence. A person decides.

**Eastside Community Health Clinic**

- AI may only draft or help someone look things up. People decide who gets care.
- A person decides sliding fee eligibility from the documents.

**Northgate Community Food Bank**

- AI never decides who gets food, SNAP help, a volunteer role or a job.
- SNAP staff stay in charge of every application.

## Being open about AI

**Bright Futures After School**

- Label translated newsletters as machine-translated, so parents know to ask if something looks wrong.
- We tell parents before kids use AI chatbots in our program.

**Cedar Valley Family Center**

- Label teaching materials made with AI.
- Answer funders honestly when they ask about AI, including in grant writing.
- Donor letters need no AI label if a person signed and meant them.

**Eastside Community Health Clinic**

- Tell patients when the scribe is listening. They can say no; use the verbal consent script.
- If we ever add a website chatbot, it must say it is not a person.

**Northgate Community Food Bank**

- Follow the AI clause in any partner agreement. If asked about our AI use, answer honestly.
