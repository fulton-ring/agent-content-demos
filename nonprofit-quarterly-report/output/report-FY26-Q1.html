How these measures are defined

  • Enrolled. Participants with at least one record this quarter. Everyone listed counts, including rows with problems.
  • Average daily attendance. School days attended divided by school days enrolled. For the organization, each site's rate weighted by its enrollment, because summary-form sites send only a rate.
  • Chronically absent. Missed 10% or more of the school days they were enrolled, for any reason, among students enrolled 10 or more days (Attendance Works; U.S. Department of Education Full-Service Community Schools guidance).
  • 45+ program hours. Share of participants with 45 or more hours of program time this quarter. The bands follow the 21st CCLC annual performance report.
  • On track. Met the reading benchmark (grades K-8) or on track for credits (grades 9-12), among students assessed.
  • Family engaged. A family member attended at least one family event or contact this quarter.
  • Attendance improved. Of students at or below 90% attendance last quarter, the share whose attendance rate went up this quarter (modeled on the 21st CCLC attendance measure). Needs student-level data from both quarters.
