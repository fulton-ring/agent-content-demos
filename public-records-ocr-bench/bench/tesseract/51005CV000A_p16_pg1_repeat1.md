To supplement the Falling Spring record, data from the USGS gaging stations on
Potts Creek (Gage Number 02013000) and Dunlap Creek (Gage Number
02014000) have been used (U.S. Department of the Interior, 1964). These creeks
are major tributaries which enter the Jackson River within the study area. A
partial record has also been maintained by the West Virginia Pulp and Paper
Company on a staff gage at its plant since about 1900. In January 1966, the
National Weather Service installed a staff gage in the same vicinity.
